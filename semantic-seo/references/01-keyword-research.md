# Module 1: Keyword Research (Entity-Based & Topical Authority)

## Philosophy
Keyword research in Semantic SEO is not about finding the most searched keyword.
It's about mapping **all the questions users have about a topic** and organizing them by entity and intent.

The output is a **Topical Map** — a hierarchy from macro to micro intents that becomes your site's content blueprint.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1 — Identify Core Topics and Entities
- Define the primary topic and the main **entity** (person, place, concept, product)
- For "electric cars": entities = *electric vehicles*, *Tesla*, *lithium-ion batteries*, *EPA*
- For local SEO: entities = *service type* + *city/neighborhood*
- Focus on the full topic, not a single keyword

### Step 2 — Gather Seed Keywords
Tools to use:
- Google Keyword Planner
- Ahrefs Keywords Explorer
- SEMrush Keyword Magic
- Moz Keyword Explorer

Start broad (1-2 words), then narrow down. Note high-volume AND highly-relevant terms.

### Step 3 — Expand via SERP Features
- **Google Autocomplete** → long-tail variations
- **People Also Ask (PAA)** → questions users want answered
- **Related Searches** → at the bottom of results
- **YouTube Autocomplete** → how-to ideas
- **Bing/Yandex suggestions** → often surface different variations

These reveal semantically related queries that tools miss.

### Step 4 — Mine Knowledge Sources and Forums
- **Wikipedia** → read section headings and linked articles (reveals topic structure)
- **Wikidata** → shows how the topic is semantically classified
- **Reddit, Quora, StackExchange** → real user questions in natural language
- **Google News** → trending subtopics, emerging entities (laws, events, people)
- **Google Knowledge Panel** → key entities Google associates with the topic
- "Users also searched for" → reveals entity associations

### Step 5 — Group by Intent
Classify every keyword into one of these intents:
| Intent | Description | Example |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| Informational | Questions, how-tos, guides | "how do electric cars work" |
| Navigational | Brand/site-specific | "Tesla electric car models" |
| Transactional | Ready to buy or act | "buy electric car online" |
| Comparative/Commercial | Best-of, vs, comparisons | "electric vs hybrid cars" |

Then group further by **subtopic/theme** (e.g., all queries about charging in one group, all about maintenance in another). Each group = one future page or section.

### Step 6 — Map the Topical Hierarchy
Arrange groups from broad to narrow:
- Macro-topic: "Electric Cars Guide"
  - Micro-topic: "Charging electric cars at home"
  - Micro-topic: "Electric car battery lifespan"
  - Micro-topic: "Cost of owning an electric car"

Cover **What, Who, How, Where, When, Why** for the main topic. If any of these is unaddressed, research that gap.

### Step 7 — Prioritize and Refine
- Prioritize by relevance to business goals + search demand
- **Do NOT skip low-volume queries if they complete the topic** — they strengthen topical authority
- Merge overlapping groups; split overly broad ones
- Produce a final **Topical Map** (spreadsheet or mind map): main topic → subtopics → supporting keywords

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Used at least 3 different research sources (tool + SERP features + wiki/forum)
- [ ] Covered definitions, how-tos, benefits/drawbacks, comparisons, FAQs
- [ ] Labeled intent for every keyword group
- [ ] Identified key entities across all keywords
- [ ] Mapped macro and micro intents in a hierarchy
- [ ] No facet of the topic left without a keyword group
- [ ] Checked SERPs for competitive content format signals
- [ ] Set a schedule for quarterly keyword refresh

---

## Key Principles

**Topical depth > volume**
A low-volume query that fills a gap in topic coverage matters more than chasing extra volume in an already-covered area.

**Entity-first mindset**
When you see a keyword, ask: what entity does this involve? Map entities, not just phrases.

**Update regularly**
Topics evolve. New PAAs appear. New entities become relevant. Keyword research is ongoing.

---

## Local SEO Application

For neighborhood-level local SEO sites:
- Core entity = [service] in [city]
- Micro-entities = each neighborhood (bairro) + its characteristics
- Groups = one group per neighborhood, one group per service variation
- Topical Map = grid of [neighborhoods] × [services]
- Intent for all = navigational + local transactional ("eletricista [bairro]")
